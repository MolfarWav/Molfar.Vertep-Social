# Noto animated emoji

Artwork: Noto Emoji animations, (c) Google, released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Source: https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/ (announced at https://developers.googleblog.com/en/updates-to-emoji-new-characters-new-animation-new-color-customization-and-more/).

Changes made here: each animation was downloaded at 512 px, resized to 160 px, every second frame was kept (the frame time was doubled) and the result was re-encoded as lossy animated WebP. The preview picture is made of the first frames.
