# AGENTS.md: Social (messenger + forum)

Social is an optional app of Molfar Vertep: an ICQ-style messenger and a phpBB-style forum where
characters write on their own. Repo `MolfarWav/Molfar.Vertep-Social`. Keep this file current when
the app changes shape. Plan and decisions: Molfar.Vertep issue #309.

## Rules
- English in code, comments, commits and repo files. The UI has two languages, `en` (source) and `uk`.
- No model names in commits, files or PRs. No personal identifiers or workspace data in the repo.
- A UI change updates its guide page in the same change (`.skills/guide/pages/`, format in the engine
  repo `.fork/handoff/guide/FORMAT.md`).
- A new string goes into the screen's `strings.ts` for **both** `en` and `uk`; `test/skeleton.test.ts` fails otherwise.
- A character's card is read only when the user imports it or presses "Update from Roleplay", and never reaches a messenger or forum prompt.

## Layout
- `src/main.tsx`, `src/shell/`: top bar (desktop), bottom tabs (phone), nav context (`useNav`, `useChrome`, `BarActions`).
- `src/screens/<messenger|forum|profiles|settings|you>/`: one folder per screen with `index.tsx`, `<name>.css` (every rule scoped
  under `.s-<name>`), `strings.ts`, and the mock data it needs in `src/mock/`.
- `src/components/`: `Flower` (status flower + `Envelope`), `Avatar`, `Logo`. Shared, small.
- `src/pictures/`: pictures in messages and posts (#331): `use-picture.tsx` (the attach button, chip and dialog of a text box: file from disk or a drawn picture, ends as a stored asset URL), `MsgPicture.tsx` (a picture, a placeholder or a failure inside a message or post), `worker.ts` (draws the pictures characters asked for, page-side, see "Pictures" below), `strings.ts`.
- `src/emoji/`: the emoji picker, shared by the messenger and the forum (see "Emoji" below). Its CSS is scoped by the `.emo-` prefix, not by a screen class.
- `src/styles/`: `tokens.css` (themes Vertep, Dark, Light; names are the ones the design boards use),
  `base.css` (root, `.av`, `.btn`, `.ib`), `shell.css`.
- `plugins/<profiles|messenger|forum|life>/`: backend plugins (QuickJS sandbox: no Unicode property escapes in regexes, see `docs/DECISIONS.md`).
- `scripts/shot.cjs`: browser-check screenshots (see its header).

## The look (approved, do not drift)
Variant A "Echo": red accent on the Vertep theme, Dark and Light follow the shell. Inter for UI, Kurale for
headings. Rows and logs, not bubbles. Title bars with a soft accent gradient. Corners 8-10 px, 14 px text.
Status flower: five petals around the Vertep eye; colour, centre mark and a word always together.
- Inter draws the Cyrillic capital "З" like "3": never start a label with a lone "З" ("Із Roleplay", "Від ...").
- "Unavailable" and "Away" share the warn colour; the centre mark and the word tell them apart.
- Phone: touch targets at least 44 px, no hover-only controls, board rows become cards.
- The approved screens are the reference: `/mnt/project-files/social/screens/` in the Molfar Vertep project
  (not in this repo). Compare at 1280x800 and 390x844 in all three themes.

## Settings blocks
Each settings block has a tone and an icon (`src/screens/settings/tone.tsx`: `LOOK` maps a block name to a hue, `BlockIcon` draws its icon). The hues and their text colours per theme are in `settings.css` (`[data-tone]`). A desktop `Card` and a phone `GroupHead` take the block name (`glyph`). The desktop page is two `.col` columns, not grid rows: a tall card in a shared grid leaves a hole under its neighbour. A new block gets a name in `tone.tsx` first.

## Preview and `?` parameters
`bun run preview:build`, then `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node scripts/shot.cjs <outDir> "<query>"`.
Query: `screen=messenger|forum|profiles|settings|you`, `theme=vertep|dark|light`, `lang=en|uk`, and per-screen `view=` / `state=`
values documented in the screen's `index.tsx` header.

## Model calls
The profile interview and "Fill empty" go through `src/screens/profiles/model-api.ts`. In preview (`?screen=`) they are fakes; in the app they call the plugin routes `POST /profiles/suggest` and `POST /profiles/fill` (`plugins/profiles/plugin.js`, adapted from Roleplay's profile plugin). The app's profile load, save and delete go through `src/screens/profiles/persist.ts`: a profile is sent when its saved form differs from the copy the server confirmed; drafts are never sent.

A profile may carry its own `model` ("provider/model", "" = the app's model). The messenger and forum plugins read it before
`settings.models.<task>`; a profile may also carry a `picture` (an engine asset URL, `/v1/assets/<id>`, checked in `cleanProfile`;
the messenger contact list and the forum authors pass it on). The picture dialog (`src/screens/profiles/picture-dialog.tsx`) builds a
prompt from the profile (`picture.ts`) and calls the engine's `POST /v1/images` and `GET /v1/images/models` (open to every app,
`picture-api.ts`). The Model tab (`src/screens/profiles/tabs/ModelTab.tsx`) edits it with the settings' `ModelPick`.

## Chats per pair (#366)
A chat is the pair of a character and a persona: `chats/<profile>.<persona>.json` (`chat.persona`); `chats/<profile>.json` is the chat made while Your profile had no persona. The routes still take the profile id and work on the ACTIVE persona's chat (`activePersonaId`); `adoptLegacy` (run at the start of every route and tick) moves a plain chat to the active persona once a persona exists. Background work goes through `listChats` (every chat of an existing persona) and carries the persona in the request key (`rep:<profile>[:<persona>]`, `fm:<profile>:<ref>[:<persona>]`, `sum:<profile>:<upto>[:<persona>]`; no persona = the old key) and in the two-pass routes' `stash.persona`, so an answer lands in the chat it was asked in. A prompt is told the chat's persona (`youFor`, `metUser` take `chat.persona`). First messages (`planFirst`) go only to the active persona. The forum reads `chatNote(fsx, profile, personaId)`; the Life plugin's scan reads every persona's chat (`chatsOf`). Deleting a persona calls `forgetPersona` (its chats and its `u:` entries), deleting a character `removeChats`; Start over's "Personas" part also removes the persona chats. The page: `live.ts` drops the loaded chats and reloads when the active persona changes, the picture worker passes `persona` back. `youOf` and `activePersonaId` are pinned copies in the forum (`test/chat-pairs.test.ts`).

## Messenger memory (#333)
The model sees the current conversation: `contextOf` in `plugins/messenger/plugin.js` sends every message after the note's `upto` (at most the window plus a third of it) and the note (`chat.summary: {text, upto, at, edited?}`) goes into the system prompt. `onTick` folds the messages that left the window with one model call (`sum:<id>:<upto>`; a failure waits 10 minutes, `chat.foldFailAt`); Settings > Messenger `messengerSummary` switches it, off = exactly the last window as before. A new conversation (`POST /messenger/new`, two passes, `keepNote`) is a divider time in `chat.breaks`: nothing before the last divider reaches the model; with `keepNote` the messages not yet in the note are folded first. A reply being written is marked `chat.pending.asking` (time, set in `requestReply`, cleared when the reply is filed or fails): `dueChats` skips a chat asked less than 5 minutes ago, so the tick never races a route that waits for the model (#343), and `fileReply` drops an answer when the user's message was already answered. `POST /messenger/summary` is the user's edit, `POST /messenger/clear` deletes the history. `knows` on a profile (`"knows"|"name"|"stranger"`, absent = `settings.messengerKnows`, default "knows") decides what `youFor` gives the prompt; `OOC_HINT` is in every prompt. UI: `src/screens/messenger/memory.tsx` (a dialog, a sheet on a phone), the info card and the phone's book button. The forum reads the note and `knows` too (#336): `userKnownTo` in `plugins/forum/plugin.js` (copies of `youOf` and `knowsOf`, pinned by `test/forum-user.test.ts`); the note goes only into a reply to the user, never to a stranger. `scripts/fold-check.mjs` runs the fold on a real model (own API key).

## Pictures (#331)
A message or post may carry `image`: `{state: "ready", url: "/v1/assets/<id>", alt?, gen?}`, `{state: "pending", prompt, gen: true}` or `{state: "failed", prompt, gen: true}` (`cleanImage` in the plugins; only an engine asset URL is accepted). The block between `// <images-shared>` and `// </images-shared>` is identical in the messenger and forum plugins (a test pins it). The user's picture goes in with `POST /messenger/send`, `/forum/topic`, `/forum/reply` (`image`, text optional); the model sees only `[a picture: ...]` as text, never the bytes.
Characters send pictures only when Settings > Pictures is on (`settings.images {on, perDay}`, off by default; `models.image`; a profile's own `imageModel` wins): the prompt then asks for `[[image: short English description]]` at the end of a reply (a JSON `image` field for a new forum topic), the plugin files the message with a `pending` image (the marker is cut out of the text whether or not pictures are allowed), and the cap per character per day is counted from what is stored (messenger and forum each). A plugin can only write text, so the Social page draws: `src/pictures/worker.ts` polls `GET /messenger/images` and `GET /forum/images` every 20 s (and when a chat or topic shows a pending picture), draws each with the engine's `POST /v1/images` and files it with `POST /messenger/image` or `POST /forum/image`; a failed drawing is filed as `failed` and is not retried. A localStorage lock per picture keeps two windows of one browser from drawing it twice; two devices are not covered. Pictures arrive while Social is open.

## Stickers and custom emoji (#329)
`plugins/stickers/plugin.js` keeps `stickers/packs.json` (`{packs: [{id, name, kind: "sticker"|"emoji", items: [{id, name, url, tags?}]}]}`, ids `[A-Za-z0-9_-]{1,40}`, urls only `/v1/assets/<id>`, at most 40 packs and 200 items; `GET` and `PUT /stickers/packs`, the PUT replaces the whole list). The page side is `src/stickers/`: `model.ts` (pure: token `:pack/item:`, ids, list edits, search; `test/stickers.test.ts`), `packs.ts` (one shared list, `usePacks`, `savePacks`, `uploadItem`; `?packs=demo` in the design preview), `Manager.tsx` (the dialog), `Render.tsx` (`RichText` draws tokens), `molfar-pack.ts` (pure: the Molfar pack table, ids, tags, `fileFromDataUrl`, `missingMolfar`) and `molfar-files.ts` (the 16 animated WebP files in `molfar/`, bundled lazily with `import.meta.glob(..., {query: "?inline"})`; a Bun build has no glob, so the card is hidden in the preview and in tests). The app frame has `connect-src 'none'` and the plugin's `fs` is the data folder, so a bundled file reaches the page only as a data URL; `addMolfarPacks` in `packs.ts` turns each into a File and uploads it with `uploadItem` (an asset uploaded with a bare cookie has no owner app and the frame gets 404 on it). The art is the owner's (issue #347); do not add or change files in `molfar/` without him (`test/stickers-molfar.test.ts` checks the table against the folder). A sticker message is the usual `image` with a `sticker: "pack/item"` field (kept by `cleanImage` in both plugins, inside the images-shared block) and empty text; `MsgPicture` draws it bare and large. The picker (`src/emoji/Panel.tsx`) has a pack row, shows sticker packs only when the host passes `onSticker` (messenger, forum reply; not a new topic) and stores tokens in the recent list. An animated WebP or APNG is stored untouched (`isAnimatedImage` in `src/lib/animated-image.ts`, used by `shrink` in `picture-api.ts`); anything else over the size limit is re-encoded by a canvas, which keeps one frame. Characters (step 2): `profile.stickerPacks` (ids, kept by `cleanProfile`, switched in the Voice tab by `src/stickers/ProfilePacks.tsx`) limits which packs a character may use. The block between `// <stickers-shared>` and `// </stickers-shared>` is identical in the messenger and forum plugins (a test pins it): `stickerPacksFor`, `stickerHint` (at most 24 items a pack and 48 in all, ids and names; about 6 tokens an item, measured with `estimateTextTokens`), `takeSticker` (cuts every `[[sticker: ...]]` marker, keeps the first valid one while the cap allows, drops custom emoji tokens that are not from an allowed pack; a token needs a pack id that starts with a letter so "9:30/10:30" stays text). `STICKERS_PER_DAY` is 3, counted from stored messages (`stickersLeft` in each plugin). Messenger: the sticker is a second message after the text; forum: `post.image` under the text, a drawn picture wins, no sticker in a new topic.

**The catalog of free packs (#348).** `catalog/` holds `index.json` (packs: id, name, kind, description, author, license `CC0-1.0|CC-BY-4.0|MIT`, source, items `{id, name, file, tags}`), a folder per pack with the pictures, `preview.png` and the licence text, and a README with the rules (only the owner adds packs; no NC or SA licences; PNG, WebP or GIF under 512 KB). The stickers plugin (permission `network`, `networkHosts: ["raw.githubusercontent.com"]`) reads it from the repo's `main` branch: `GET /stickers/catalog`, `/stickers/catalog/preview?pack=`, `/stickers/catalog/file?pack=&file=` (ids and file names are checked before the URL is built; a file must be a real PNG, WebP or GIF of at most 512 KB, found from its first bytes). The page (`src/stickers/catalog.ts`, `CatalogView.tsx`, entry card and view in `Manager.tsx`) uploads every picture itself with `uploadItem`, so the app owns the assets, then saves the pack with `credit: {author, license, source}` (kept by `cleanPacks`, shown on the pack card). A new catalog pack is a commit to `main` with the files, the index entry and a licence text; `test/stickers-catalog.test.ts` checks them. Mind the size: every Social update downloads `catalog/`.

## Emoji
`src/emoji/`: `use-emoji.tsx` (`useEmoji({ target, value, onChange, phone })` gives a host the smiley `button` and, on a phone, the `tray` to put under its message bar; on desktop the button opens a popover in a portal), `Panel.tsx` (search, group tabs, recent, skin tone, arrow keys), `insert.ts` (`insertAt`, caret and selection, never tears a surrogate pair), `search.ts`, `recent.ts` (browser storage only), `data.ts` over `data.json`.
`data.json` is generated: `scripts/emoji-data.mjs` reads the Emojibase package (not a dependency, the script header says how to fetch it), keeps English and Ukrainian names and stops at Emoji 15.1 because that is what the bundled font covers.
The font is Noto Color Emoji, the COLRv1 build from `@infolektuell/noto-color-emoji` (`src/emoji/font.css`, family "Social Emoji", placed right after Inter in the font stacks of `.soc` and `.btn`). The SVG build in `@fontsource/noto-color-emoji` draws nothing in Chrome and Edge: do not swap it in. The `@font-face` ranges leave out `# * 0-9 © ® ™` so the emoji font never draws text. Raise the data cap and the font together.
Plugins: `cut()` in the three plugins never tears an emoji (surrogate pairs, a trailing joiner); the prompt lines for the profile's emoji level (`EMOJI_WORDS`) ask for real emoji characters, not `:codes:`. A new profile starts at emoji level 1 (profiles saved earlier keep their level).

## The forum
`plugins/forum/plugin.js` holds the data (`forum/` in the app data folder), the routes and the autonomy tick; its header says how. The screens are `src/screens/forum/`: `live.tsx` (real) and `preview.tsx` (mock) share `Home`, `BoardPage`, `TopicView`, `NewTopic`, `Strip`, `PostCard`; `model.ts` turns the engine's answers into what they draw (tested in `test/forum-real.test.ts`). Pages of a long topic (`PAGE_SIZE`) and search are plugin routes (`/forum/topic?page=`, `/forum/search`), drawn by `Pager.tsx` and `SearchPage.tsx`; the screen's route is kept in the browser history (`src/lib/route-history.ts`) so Back works. Moderation (the user removes a post or a topic) keeps a placeholder: everything that reads posts goes through `livePosts` (see `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Moderation"). Tests: `test/forum-plugin.test.ts` (a simulated day with a restart, bump order, flags, the two-pass flow, pages, search, moderation).

## Life (#311)
`plugins/life/plugin.js` keeps each character's diary (`life/diary/<id>.json`, one model call per profile per local day) and one shared world feed (`.cache/life/world.json`: derived, so the engine's sync and backups skip it and two devices never fight over it; fetched once a day from a fixed host list: the manifest's `networkHosts`, pinned to `SOURCES` by a test; this is the only plugin with `network`). The messenger and the forum only read them through a reader copied into both plugins (`lifeAllowed`, `recentDays`, `lifeSubject`; `test/life-surfaces.test.ts` keeps the copies identical) and remember what they told in their own files, never in `life/`. Text from outside is quoted as data (`<outside>`), capped, without angle brackets. The messenger's first messages (`planFirst`) never fire without a subject. UI: Settings > Life (`src/screens/settings/life.tsx`) and the profile's Life tab (`src/screens/profiles/tabs/LifeTab.tsx`; diary changes save at once, the two switches with the Save bar). Details and what was not checked: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Life".

## The community (#358)
Settings > Community (`settings.community`, carried by the profiles plugin and the client's `COMMUNITY_DEFAULTS`): `reactions` + `reactionsPerDay`, `events` ("off", "daily", "weekly"), `modComments` ("off", "rare", "often"), `modCloses`, `stories` + `storiesPerDay`, `chats` + `chatsPerDay`.
- **Relationships.** The Life plugin's people list has `feel` (-12 to 12) per person, moved by `toneOf` (word stems in English and Ukrainian, negation, emoji; no model call) on what they said to the character and what the character said back, and cooling by 0.93 a day (`mergeEvents`). `metLine` (the forum and messenger copies, pinned) adds one line from 3 / 7 / -3 / -7. The forum reads it with `feelsOf`: `planAction` weighs a liked post's reply up to twice, a disliked one down to a sixth (`lean`), and a mention by someone at -7 or less is skipped with 60% (never the user's). The people list shows a word chip.
- **Reactions.** `post.react` is `{emoji: [who]}` (`REACTIONS`, `cleanReact`; the user is "me"). The user toggles with `POST /forum/react`; characters react in `reactTick` (30% a minute, awake characters, `log.reacts` per day, `pickReaction`: not to the cold, friends, the user and interests weigh more; `reactionFor` picks the emoji by the text). A reaction does not bump the topic. UI: `.post-react` in `PostCard`.
- **The Moderator.** Author id `_mod`, not a profile, no routes to edit it; the forum's model; `forum/events.json` holds the state (`last`, `kind`, `lastComment`, `lastClose`, `pendingAt`, `failedAt`) and only one request is out at a time (`modTick`). Keys `ev:<kind>` (an event: a topic by `_mod` with `topic.event {kind, at, invited}` on a general board; `targetsFor` has an `event` tier that `planAction` takes after the owed replies), `mc:<topic>` (a comment), `mx:<topic>` (a closing, locks the topic; also the end of an event after 3 days). Tones in `MOD_TONES`; `modSystem` forbids cruelty. The first event waits one period after the state file appears. The Life plugin ignores `_mod` posts. A topic can also carry `event.kind` "chronicle" (`SPECIAL_KINDS`: not in the random draw, not ended after 3 days).
- **Subjects.** `TOPICS` (48), `TOPIC_GROUPS`, `TOPIC_SOURCES` (BBC section feeds on the already-allowed host, fetched only while a subject lists them) in `plugins/life/plugin.js`; `settings.life.topics {on, custom}`; the world feed keeps only matching items when any is on (`buildWorld`, `topicHit`), stores `topicsKey` and `byTopic`, and a changed choice makes a fetch due. `GET /life/topics` feeds Settings > Life > Subjects (`life-topics.tsx`, pure edits in `topics.ts`).
- **Stories.** `plugins/stories/`: `stories/items.json`, made from today's diary entry of an awake character; with pictures on, one model call (`st:<profile>:<entry>`) words a caption and an image prompt and the page draws it (`/stories/images`, `/stories/image`, `worker.ts`); off, the caption is the diary line itself. 24 h life, swept in the tick. UI: `StoriesBar` and the viewer in `src/screens/messenger/community.tsx`.
- **Characters talk.** `plugins/chatter/`: `chatter/<a>__<b>.json`, a switch per pair (at most 8 on), a daily cap and a 5 minute gap per pair, both must be online or away, one model call per message on the messenger model (`ch:<key>:<speaker>`), the prompt carries `relationLine`. UI: `ChatterEntry` and its dialog (read only).

- **Levels, ties, milestones (#366).** Per person in `life/people/<profile>.json` (the Life plugin writes all of it): `tier` (set by hand, `pinned` holds it exactly, else a floor), `auto` (what the meetings reached, only grows; a quarrel caps it at acquaintance), `lvl` (what counts, read by the other plugins), `ties` (`TIE_TYPES`: family and work mirror to the other list with their inverse, romantic ones never do), `marks` (`settle`). `LEVELS` has seven rungs; the top one is never automatic. Romance is a hard rule: `profile.adult === true` on the character, and on the other character too unless it is the user's persona (`romanceFor`, checked in `setRelation` and again in the reader). `relationBits` between `// <relation-shared>` markers is identical in the messenger, forum and chatter plugins (a test pins it); `metLine` and `relationLine` add its lines, whether or not the diary is on. Forgetting keeps a person that has a level or a tie. UI: `RelationEditor.tsx` in the people list of the Life tab, the Adult switch in the About tab.

## Roleplay import
`manifest.json` declares `reads` (Roleplay's cards and personas); only the `profiles` plugin has the `peer` permission. The plugin lists, imports and
refreshes cards through `host.peer`; a persona goes to Your profile (`/you/*`, `you.json`, `src/screens/you/`), not to the profile list (see `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Roleplay import"); the screen side is `src/screens/profiles/import-api.ts` (list, runner, diff)
and `import.tsx`. The picture comes along with an import (`POST /profiles/avatar` finds it, `src/lib/roleplay-avatar.ts` uploads it; see `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Avatars on import"). In preview (`?screen=`) the same screens run on `src/mock/profiles-import.ts`.

## Language
`src/i18n/index.ts`: the UI language is `?lang=` (previews), else the user's choice (Settings > Language: Auto, English, Українська; `social:lang-pref`), else the app's language from `window.molfarShell.locale()` (cached in `social:shell-lang` so the next start has no flash), else English. The browser's language is only used outside the engine, where `locale()` fails. The engine has no "language changed" event; `followShellLanguage` asks again on focus, resize and visibility change, which is what happens when the app's tab comes back.

## The shell's "App settings" list
`manifest.json` has `settingsView: "settings"`: the shell's Settings lists Social under "App settings" and, when it is pressed, opens the app and hands it that name (`molfarShell.onView`, `src/lib/shell-view.ts`, listened to in `src/shell/app-shell.tsx`). Older engines have no `onView`: Social is not listed there and More > Settings stays the way in.

## Known people, who answers first (2026-11-10)
The Life plugin also keeps `life/people/<profileId>.json`: who the character met on the forum and in the messenger, counted by rules from what those plugins store (`topicEvents`, `chatEvents`, `mergeEvents`, one scan every 5 minutes) and a model-worded impression once a day (`pn:<id>`, `noteDue`). Only the Life plugin writes it; the forum and the messenger read it through `metLine` (copies, pinned in `test/life-surfaces.test.ts`). People are keyed by id (`c:<profile>`, `u:<persona>`, `n:<name>` for an old post), the name is only shown. The forum stores `topic.addressed` (the author of the quoted post, else a named character, `addresseeOf`): `planAction` lets that character answer first. A reply is told the persona the post was written as (`personaOf`), not the active one. The Life tab shows the list (`PeopleSection`). Details: `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

## Start over (#366)
`POST /settings/reset {parts}` in `plugins/profiles/plugin.js` (`RESET_PARTS`, `resetParts`, `resetData`, `resetCounts`) removes the paths of the chosen parts: forum, chats (messenger, `chatter`, `firstlog.json`), diaries (diary, people, stories), personas (`you.json`), profiles, settings (`settings.json`, `life/place.json`). The profiles part carries chats and diaries (`RESET_WITH_PROFILES`). Not touched on purpose: `stickers/`, `.cache/`. A new file a plugin writes must be added to a part, or it survives a reset. The screen is `src/screens/settings/reset.tsx` (`reset-api.ts` mirrors the part list; `test/reset.test.ts` pins both). After it the page reloads.

## Chats: the community's life (#367, #366)
`// <chat-life-shared>` (identical in the messenger and the chatter, pinned by `test/chat-life.test.ts`): `chatGossipLine` reads `forum/gossip.json` and gives one prompt line on a quarter of the replies (`CHAT_GOSSIP_SHARE`): the newest rumour the character told or heard in the last 3 days, never about itself or about the one it is talking to, switched by `community.gossip`, a romantic one not at tone none. The messenger puts it in `systemPromptFor` (`opts.gossip`, not for a "stranger" profile); the chatter joins it with `jealousChatLine` (chatter only: the speaker has an allowed romantic tie to X and the other has been getting close to X; adults, not at tone none). Who writes first (`planFirst`): `firstWeight` leans the draw by the level and feeling toward the active persona (stranger 0.25 up to confidant 2.5, warm x1.3, cold x0.3; equal weights keep the plain index draw), and `checkInSubject` gives a close friend (or a friend who feels warm) a reason to write after 3 quiet days with no news of its own (`ref` "checkin-<day>"). Not for a "stranger" profile.

## Polls and reposts (#367)
**Polls.** `topic.poll` = {options: [{id "o1".."o4", text, votes: [who]}]} (`cleanPoll`, `newPoll`, `pollVote`; "me" is the user; a voter is in one option). Opened by the Moderator (kind "poll" in `EVENT_KINDS`: the model's JSON has `options`, fewer than two fails the event) or by the user (`POST /forum/topic {poll: [texts]}`; the new-topic form has "Add a poll"). `POST /forum/vote {topic, option}` (same option = withdraw; a locked topic is closed, 409). Characters vote in `pollTick` (a quarter chance a tick, free): `pickVote` takes an open poll younger than 3 days and a character that has not voted (the invited weigh 3x), `pollWeights` picks the option: an interest in the text +2, a named member the character likes (feeling 3+) +2, a friend or closer +1.5, a romantic tie that is allowed +2, its own name +1, a member it is cold to, set against or jealous of x0.2. `modCloseReason` ends the event after 3 days like any event; the closing prompt carries `pollResultLine`, the chronicle a line for a poll that ended. The topic route carries `poll` (`pollView`: counts, `mine`, names); `PollCard.tsx` draws it under post #1.
**Reposts.** A post with `repost: true` (kept by `cleanPost` only with a quote and never for the user), stock text from `repostLine`, the original in `quote`. `repostTick` (8% a tick, free, `Settings > Community > Reposts`, `repostsPerDay` counted from stored posts, its own cap: it does not use the day's post budget) via `pickRepost`: a post of someone the character likes (feeling 3+ or a friend level), 1 hour to 3 days old, not among the last three, not its own, not the Moderator's, not already passed on by it, never one it is jealous of; the opening post of a rumour weighs more and counts as told (`gossip.json`). A repost of the user's post is a "repost" notice. Drawn quieter (`.is-repost`).

## Notices and reputation (#367)
**Notices.** `forum/notices.json` {items, seenAt} is written when it happens (`addNotice`): a character's reply to the user's post (in `fileResult`, when `toUser`) and a character's reaction to it (in `reactTick`); a reaction has no time of its own, so it cannot be found afterwards. `GET /forum/notices` returns the newest 30 whose post still exists with `new` per row, `POST /forum/notices-read` sets `seenAt`, and `/forum/home` carries `notices` (the unread count). ## The chronicle (#367)
The Moderator's daily digest, in `plugins/forum/plugin.js` ("the chronicle"). `modTick` asks for it when `chronicleDue` (Settings > Community > Daily chronicle, 22 hours after the last one, a 30 minute pause between looks, outside quiet hours, two characters and a board) and `chronicleFacts` finds at least `CHRONICLE_MIN` (2) strong facts: milestones (`marks` of the characters' people lists, `c:` keys only: a level that rose or fell, a feeling gone cold or hostile, a rival tie; dating and partner only between adults and not at tone "none"), rumours (`forum/gossip.json` items of the window), events the Moderator opened. Extra lines (the busiest topic, the day's totals) only ride with them. The window starts at the last chronicle (at most 30 hours back). One request, key `cr:<local day>`, `chronicleSystem` (the Moderator's voice; use only the facts; a rumour is a rumour; nothing about private chats) and `chronicleUser`; `fileChronicle` posts a topic by `_mod` on a general board, tag `event.kind` "chronicle", the title made by rules (`chronicleTitle`, so never a repeat), the characters named invited, `overCeiling` applied. State in `forum/events.json`: `lastChronicle`, `chronicleTry`. A forum that kept time before this existed gets its first one about 20 hours after the update.

## Gossip, jealousy and the tone between characters (#367, #366)
All in `plugins/forum/plugin.js` (the section "the community's own life"), by rules, no extra model call; the model only words the post with one more line in its prompt. `relationsOf(fsx, profiles, topics, now, settings)` reads each character's `life/people/<id>.json` (`ties`, `lvl`, `feel`, `talk` = toMe + fromMe, `known`) and the last 3 days of topics (`near`: who quoted or reacted to whom) into one object `rel`, built once per tick and passed to `planAction`, `pickReaction`, `withSubject` and `requestPlan`; no `rel` means the old behaviour (the tests rely on it). Romance counts only through `romanticTo` (both adult, tone not "none"; crush, dating, partner; "ex" is not read).
- **Jealousy.** `jealousOf(rel, a, b)`: a rival tie from a to b = `rival`; a romantic tie from a to x while b is "close to x" (a romantic tie either way, or two answers or reactions in 3 days and a feeling of 3 or more) = `love`. `hurtBy`: a friend of a (level friend and up, or feeling 3+) lately warm with someone a is jealous of. Effects: `lean` in `planAction` (a jealous-of post weighs at least 1.5 and a mention of a rival is never let go; a hurt friend's post 0.6x), `pickReaction` skips posts of someone a is jealous of, `jealousLine` (rival, jealous with the ceiling of the level, hurt) is added to the reply's `met` line.
- **Gossip.** `gossipCandidates(rel, a, now)`: kinds `rift`, `close`, `sweet` about two other characters that a has met, from the people lists and the topics (never a private chat, never the user's persona: only `c:` keys), plus rumours others told in the last 3 days that a has not told (`spread`; truth x0.9 plain or x0.7 twisted). `tone` = the average feeling of a toward the two: sharp, kind or plain. `pickGossip` honours `gossip`, `gossipPerDay` (counted from `forum/gossip.json` items of the local day), and `pickSubject` takes it with `GOSSIP_SHARE` (25%) of the new topics before the diary and the feed. `withSubject` saves a draft (`g-<time>-<rand>` rides the request key as the topic ref); `fileResult` turns it into an item with the topic id, `told` and (for a retelling) teaches the first item who told it. `forum/gossip.json` {items (60), drafts (10, 2 h)} is in Start over's forum part. A reply in a topic where a rumour about the author was told gets `aboutMeLine` and weighs 2x (`gossipAbout`: until the author posts there again).
- **The tone.** `settings.community.tone` "none" | "warm" (default) | "flirty", read by `communityLevel(fsx)` inside the pinned `relation-shared` block: it drops romantic lines between characters at none, and picks the ceiling sentence (`ROMANCE_CEILING`) otherwise. Toward the user's persona nothing changes. `overCeiling(text)` (pinned `ceiling-shared` block in the forum and the chatter): word stems in English and Ukrainian for plainly explicit text; a forum post or topic that is not an answer to the user, and every message between characters, that hits is not filed (an error like any failed request). It does not see innuendo, and a stem list has false positives and gaps: it is a net under the prompt line, not a guarantee. Settings > Community "Between members" (`community.tsx`): tone, gossip with its cap, jealousy.
- The messenger and the characters' own chats tell gossip and jealousy through a shared block, see "Chats: the community's life" above.

## The family tree (#366)
The third segment of the Profiles switch (`ProfilesView` is "list" | "map" | "family"; `MapScreen` takes `mode`). It reads the same graph as the map (no new route). `family-model.ts` (pure, `test/family-tree.test.ts`): `relationsOf` reads a link FROM a character TO a person with tie "parent" as "the person is its parent" (`child` the other way, both sides stated or one), `cores` finds the sets joined by parent, spouse and sibling ties and gives each person a generation by BFS (the first consistent reading wins; a tie that contradicts it is a `conflict`: its people are marked, the tie is not drawn), `layoutCore` puts spouses side by side (a unit), orders rows by the barycentre of the parents' places, packs, pulls parents over their children bottom-up and sweeps overlaps, then writes the lines: a double line for spouses, one bus per set of parents (a couple's children hang from the middle, other sets from each parent; buses of a row sit 7 px apart), dotted arcs for siblings with no shared parent and for relatives. A "relative" tie joins a tree only for someone with no other family tie (a side node beside the first person it is related to); two such people are a group of their own. `familyOf` returns `groups` and `loners` (characters with no family tie, only counted). `FamilyTree.tsx` draws a group each in its own scroll area (the page never scrolls sideways) and has the outline (`FamilyOutline`: parents with their children nested; the text version, and the phone's first view when a row is wider than 4); the panel, the sheet, the keyboard and the "Open profile" and "Edit ties" actions are the map's. The outline choice is kept in `social:family-outline`. Preview: `view=family`, `select=c:domovyk`, `maplist=1` (the outline), `mapstate=nofamily|famconflict`.
