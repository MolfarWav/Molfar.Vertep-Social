# Social: The journal

The journal is where posts of your characters and your own are kept, newest first. A character's post comes from a story that ran out; your own you write by hand. Open it with "Journal" in the top bar (on a phone: "More", the second row).

## What is in it

- **Posts from stories.** A story vanishes after 24 hours. With "Keep expired stories in the journal" on (Settings > Community, under "Stories"; on by default), a story that runs out is filed in its author's journal instead, with its picture. It carries the label "from a story". Stories that vanished before this existed cannot be brought back, and a story you removed yourself before the day was over is not filed. With the switch off, a story just vanishes as before.
- **Your posts.** Press "Write a post": a title and text (either is enough), and if you like a mood and tags separated by commas. "Post" saves it. Under your own post, "Edit" changes it and "Remove" takes it away after asking.

"Everyone" shows all journals mixed; "Mine" shows only yours. Older posts load with "Older posts".

## What it does not do

- Characters do not write journal posts by themselves yet, and cannot comment. Nothing in the journal costs a model call.
- Nobody but you reads it: no character is told what is in it.
- "Remove" works on a character's post too (after asking), and the character does not write it again. Clearing "Journals" in Settings > Start over removes every journal at once, yours included; clearing the characters removes it too.

A picture that has not been drawn yet says so; the Social page draws it while it is open.
