# Social: Messenger

The Messenger is a chat with the characters that live in Social. You see a contact list, the conversation with the selected contact and a card with their details. Anyone who wants to talk to a character one to one starts here.

## The contact list

Contacts are grouped by status: **Online**, **Away**, **Do not disturb**, **Unavailable** and **Offline**, each with a count. Click a group's title to fold it. A flower sits beside every name. Its colour, the mark in its centre and the word in the chat header tell the status. A contact with a waiting message shows an envelope instead of the flower and a bold name.

Type in "Find a contact" to filter the list by name. If nobody matches, the list says so and offers "Clear search". Your own status ("My status") is at the bottom of the list on a desktop and on top of it on a phone. On a phone, the search icon in the top bar opens the search field.

## A conversation

Select a contact to open the chat. Your messages and theirs are lines with a coloured name and a time, not bubbles. Type in the box and press Enter to send; Shift+Enter starts a new line. "Clear" empties the box.

The status changes how a character behaves. An online character answers within seconds. An away character answers in a few minutes. A character who is offline gets the message and answers when they are back; the chat says that the message will be answered then. While a reply is on its way you see "<name> is typing…". If no reply comes, the chat tells you what went wrong and offers "Try again". If no model is chosen for the Messenger, the chat says so and links to the settings.

### Memory and a new conversation

A character sees the latest 24 messages of the chat with each reply (4 to 500 in Settings, Messenger, "Messages a character remembers"; see [[settings]]). Older messages are not lost: when they scroll out, one model call now and then folds them into a short **note** that goes to the model with the latest messages. The note is under "Memory" in the info card on a computer; the "Memory and conversation…" button there, or the book button in the chat's top bar on a phone, opens it. You can read the note, edit it and press "Save note": what you write replaces it, and the model adds to it later. "Note of older messages" in Settings switches the folding off.

In the same dialog, **New conversation…** puts a divider in the log. The character sees nothing from before it. "Keep the note and start" first folds the messages that are not in the note yet (one model call), so the character still remembers the gist; "Start without the note" starts from nothing. The old messages stay in the chat above the divider. **Clear chat…** deletes the whole history and the note, after a question; it cannot be undone.

How well a character knows you is a choice too: "Characters know you" in Settings (the default), or "How well it knows you" in a profile's Voice tab. **Knows you** tells the model the name and "About" of your active persona; **Name only** tells just the name; **Stranger** tells nothing, and the character learns about you only from what you write. A message in double parentheses, like ((this)), or one that starts with OOC is you speaking out of character: the character answers it briefly and plainly, then goes on.

The smiley button next to the message box ("Emoji") opens a picker with a search field, the groups (smileys, people, animals, food, travel, activities, objects, symbols, flags) and the emoji you used last. Click an emoji to put it at the cursor, or over the text you have selected; the picker closes and you keep typing. Hold Shift while you click to keep it open and add several. Type in "Search emoji" to find one by name in English or Ukrainian; Enter takes the first match. The six dots at the bottom ("Skin tone") change the tone of the emoji that have one, and the choice is remembered, like the recent ones. On a phone the picker opens under the message box instead of over it, and stays open until you press the cross or the smiley button again. Arrow keys move between emoji and Escape closes the picker.

Social carries its own emoji font, so emoji look the same on Windows, Android and Linux; Safari draws its own. Characters write emoji too: how often is set per character, see "Writing manner" in [[profiles]].

On a desktop the card on the right shows the schedule (when the character is online next), how fast the character answers. A character may also write first: an online character with something from its own days or from the outside world opens the chat with it (see [[life]]), within the daily limit in Settings. That message is unread until you open the chat. Whether a character writes first is not shown on the card. "Open profile" goes to their profile. On a phone a chat takes the whole screen; the arrow at the top returns to the list.

## Pictures

Next to the emoji button of the message box is a picture button. It opens "Add a picture": **Upload from disk** (PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, up to 10 MB; big pictures are scaled down to 768 px), or describe a picture and press **Draw** to have your image model draw it (it uses your image provider and may cost what that provider charges; pick the model in the dialog, the app's default is used if you pick none). Press **Attach**: a small chip shows the picture above the box, with a cross to remove it. A message may be a picture alone. A picture you send stays in Social: a character is told only that you sent a picture (and its description when you drew it), never the picture itself, so it works with text-only models.

Characters can send pictures too, but only if you switch it on in Settings, Pictures (off by default, see [[settings]]). A character then puts a picture in a message now and then when it fits, at most the daily number you set. While it is being drawn the message shows a placeholder; the picture appears when it is ready, and a small "Drawn" tag marks pictures made by a model. Characters' pictures are drawn by the Social page, so they appear while Social is open (a picture asked for while it was closed is drawn the next time you open it). If drawing fails, the message says so and no new try is made.

## When the list is empty

The contacts are the characters you import in Profiles. With none, the Messenger shows "No profiles yet" and a button that opens Profiles. See [[profiles]].
