# Social: Messenger

The Messenger is a chat with the characters that live in Social. You see a contact list, the conversation with the selected contact and a card with their details. Anyone who wants to talk to a character one to one starts here.

## The contact list

Contacts are grouped by status: **Online**, **Away**, **Do not disturb**, **Unavailable** and **Offline**, each with a count. Click a group's title to fold it. A flower sits beside every name. Its colour, the mark in its centre and the word in the chat header tell the status. A contact with a waiting message shows an envelope instead of the flower and a bold name.

Type in "Find a contact" to filter the list by name. If nobody matches, the list says so and offers "Clear search". Your own status ("My status") is at the bottom of the list on a desktop and on top of it on a phone. On a phone, the search icon in the top bar opens the search field.

## A conversation

Select a contact to open the chat. Your messages and theirs are lines with a coloured name and a time, not bubbles. Type in the box and press Enter to send; Shift+Enter starts a new line. "Clear" empties the box.

The status changes how a character behaves. An online character answers within seconds. An away character answers in a few minutes. A character who is offline gets the message and answers when they are back; the chat says that the message will be answered then. While a reply is on its way you see "<name> is typing…". If no reply comes, the chat tells you what went wrong and offers "Try again". If no model is chosen for the Messenger, the chat says so and links to the settings.

On a desktop the card on the right shows the schedule (when the character is online next), how fast the character answers. A character may also write first: an online character with something from its own days or from the outside world opens the chat with it (see [[life]]), within the daily limit in Settings. That message is unread until you open the chat. Whether a character writes first is not shown on the card. "Open profile" goes to their profile. On a phone a chat takes the whole screen; the arrow at the top returns to the list.

## When the list is empty

The contacts are the characters you import in Profiles. With none, the Messenger shows "No profiles yet" and a button that opens Profiles. See [[profiles]].
