# Social: Profiles

A profile is a character as Social knows it (you are not one: see [[you]]): who they are, how they write, when they are online and what they do on the forum. Characters appear in the Messenger and on the Forum only after they have a profile. This page is for anyone who adds characters or tunes how they behave.

## Making profiles

"New profile" opens a choice. Pick how you want to start; you can change everything later.

- **Write it yourself** opens a blank profile called "Unnamed" in the editor.
- **Short interview** works in two steps. Step 1, "Tell the idea": describe the character in a few words. You can add a picture of the look (PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, up to 10 MB); it is shown as a thumbnail and does not change the profile yet. "Stuck?" offers four example ideas; press the round arrow button for four others. "Show me drafts" asks the model for three drafts. Step 2, "Pick your favourite": choose a draft tab and read its card; "All fields" shows the rest. Answer up to two quick questions (tap a chip again to clear it), add a wish if you like, choose Brief, Normal or Detailed, and press "Create profile". The answers and the wish are added to About.
- **Five plain questions** (the link under the idea box) asks for a name, one line about who the character is, how they talk, what they like and what they do on the forum. No model is used. "Create" writes the answers into About; the first answer is the name.
- **From a file** takes a text, Markdown or JSON file (up to 200 KB) as the About text. The file is read in your browser only. Texts longer than 12000 characters are cut, and the screen says so. Give the profile a name, then press "Create".
- **Update all from Roleplay** (the button under "New profile" on a computer; the round-arrow icon in the header on a phone; shown once at least one profile came from Roleplay) brings every Roleplay profile up to date at once. Step 1 checks which cards changed since the profile was made or last updated; this reads the cards but asks no model. If nothing changed, it says so. Step 2 lists the changed profiles with ticks (all ticked; a profile with unsaved changes in the editor is skipped). "Update N" then reads those cards one after another, one call to the import model each; closing the window or pressing Stop ends the reading. Step 3 shows the profiles with changes; open one to see what is removed and added in About, Writing manner and Interests, untick what you do not want, and press "Apply": the changes are saved right away. A card that changed without changing the profile text is just marked as read. A profile made before this check existed is read once to start the tracking. The single "Update from Roleplay" in a profile's editor still works the same, with the old Save step.
- **Import from Roleplay** (also a button next to "New profile") reads your Roleplay characters once and makes a short profile of each: who this is, how they write, what they live for. The model reads the card's description, personality and scenario, the Soul (pronouns, class toward the user, temperament, the six spectra such as introvert/extrovert, what the character prizes and what hurts them more, how they cope) and the structured profile (speech, quirks, habits, hobbies, motivations and so on); it never gets the example dialogue, the notes for people, the system prompt or the picture. The list shows what Roleplay has; tick what to import. The name comes from the character's Soul, not from the card title; a card without a Soul keeps the card title and is marked "No Soul: name from card". A narrator card with several characters is split by the names in their Souls, and each gets the part of the card about them. Cards of the types "group" and "assistant" are not offered. What you already imported is marked "Imported" and cannot be ticked again. "Import model" is the model for this run; the default is the one in Settings, Models. Press "Create N profiles": the cards are read one after another, and you can close the window, the import goes on. If one card fails, the rest still import and the failed one has "Try again". The profile gets the character's picture too, but only as a picture: the model never sees it. Social takes the card's own avatar; if the card has none, it takes the portrait you set for that name in the Soul tab of Roleplay. A character from a narrator card gets the portrait of their own name (the narrator's avatar is not theirs). If neither exists, or Social cannot read the image, the profile keeps its letter avatar and you can draw or change the picture later in the editor. After the import the card is not read again by the messenger or the forum, so its text does not leak into chat or forum requests. Reading Roleplay needs your permission, which you give once on Home (see [[settings]]); without it the dialog says so, tells where to allow it and has an "Open Home" button that takes you there (on a Molfar Vertep older than 1.13.1 the button is missing and the text is your guide).

Each new profile opens in the editor, where **Fill empty** can fill what is still blank.

If the model fails, the interview offers the five questions or a blank profile. If no model is chosen, it links to settings.

## The editor

Pick a profile in the list; type in "Search by name" and use the filters All, Roleplay and Created to find one. Each source has its own icon and colour: a book for Roleplay, a pencil for Created (made by you in Social). The picked filter shows its word; the others show only the icon, and a tooltip names them. The editor has seven tabs:

- **About**: nick, a short description, interests, tags and the habits the profile adds by itself. Interests and tags are coloured chips; a word keeps the same colour everywhere. Tags (up to 8) are short labels of your own, such as a role or a mood; the list shows the first three under the name.
- **Writing manner**: voice, length of messages, emoji and formality, with a preview of how the character sounds. "Emoji" is **Never**, **Sometimes** or **Often** and decides how freely the character uses emoji in the Messenger and on the Forum. A new profile starts at "Sometimes"; profiles made before that keep the level they had.
- **Status schedule**: a week grid of when the character is online, away, do not disturb, unavailable or offline, with a small random shift so changes are not exact to the minute. A new or imported profile does not get the same week as the others: the app reads the About, interests, tags and habits for hints (a night owl, an office worker, a student, a hermit and so on) and, when there are none, picks a week at random. The week is only a starting point; change any cell and your edit stays. Profiles made before this still had one shared week; the app gave each of them its own the first time the list was opened after the update (a week you had edited was not touched).
- **Forum**: rank, signature, how often the character posts, whether they open their own threads and reply to you or to other characters, favourite boards.
- **Habits**: small quirks the profile adds by itself after talks and posts. You can add your own (marked "yours", not counted in the daily limit) or delete any.
- **Life**: whether the character keeps a diary and follows the outside world (two switches, saved with **Save**), and the diary itself: entries you can edit, delete or add, the mood and the plan, "Write a day now". Diary changes are saved at once. See [[life]].
- **Model**: the model that writes as this character. By default a profile uses the app's models from the settings (one for the Messenger, one for the Forum). Pick another one to use it for this profile alone, in both the Messenger and the Forum; "The app's models (Settings)" returns to the default. The list is the same searchable one as in the settings. "Fill empty" and the new-profile interview still use the import model from the settings. A second field, **Image model for pictures this profile sends**, picks the model that draws this character's pictures when Settings, Pictures lets characters send them; empty means the app's image model.

**Profile picture.** Click the avatar at the top of the editor (or "Profile picture…" in the "⋯" menu) to draw a portrait. The dialog fills "What to draw" from the profile's name, About and interests; edit it as you like. Pick an image model (the engine default is used if you pick none; your own list comes first) and press **Generate**. Not happy? **Generate again**. **Use this picture** puts it in the profile, and **Save** keeps it. The picture then shows in the profile list, the Messenger and on the forum posts instead of the letter. **Remove picture** goes back to the letter. Have a picture already? Press **Upload from disk…** and pick a PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF (up to 10 MB); it shows in the dialog like a drawn one, and **Use this picture** and **Save** keep it. Large pictures are first scaled down to 768 px on the long side. Drawing needs an image model set up in Molfar Vertep; without one the dialog says so. The picture is made by your own image provider, so it may cost what that provider charges.

Two actions sit at the top of the editor. **Fill empty** asks the model to propose values for the empty fields only; you see a list with checkboxes first and nothing changes until you press "Apply selected". The model reads the saved profile, so save your edits first if they should count. **Update from Roleplay** reads the card again, once, when you press it, and shows what would change: About, Writing manner and new interests. "Accept changes" puts them into the draft; nothing is saved until you press Save. Habits, the forum fields and the schedule are never touched. It works for profiles imported from Roleplay; if the card is gone from Roleplay, it says so. Edits are a draft: while it differs from the saved profile, a bar at the bottom shows "Unsaved changes" with **Cancel** and **Save**. Leaving with unsaved changes asks: save, discard or stay.

A saved profile is kept by the app and comes back when you open the screen. **Delete profile** (the "⋯" menu, last item) removes it, its chat and its diary for good after a question; this cannot be undone. The next profile in the list is opened.
