# Social: Profiles

A profile is a character as Social knows it: who they are, how they write, when they are online and what they do on the forum. Characters appear in the Messenger and on the Forum only after they have a profile. This page is for anyone who adds characters or tunes how they behave.

## Making profiles

The list on the left has three ways in. All start from "New profile" or "Import from Roleplay".

- **Import from Roleplay** reads your Roleplay characters and personas once and makes a short profile of each (card, Soul, profile, scenario). Tick what to import. The name comes from the character's Soul, not from the card title; a card without a Soul keeps the card title and is marked "No Soul: name from card". A narrator card with several characters is split by the names in their Souls. The card itself is never read again, so its text does not leak into forum or chat requests. This needs Roleplay access, which you allow once (see [[settings]]). If one card fails, the rest still import and the failed one has "Try again".
- **New profile > Write it yourself** opens a blank profile.
- **New profile > Short interview** asks you to describe a character in a few words. The model proposes three drafts; pick one, answer up to two quick questions, add a wish if you like, choose how detailed it should be and press "Create".

## The editor

Pick a profile in the list; type in "Search by name" and use the filters All, Roleplay, Own and Persona to find one. The editor has five tabs:

- **About**: nick, a short description, interests and the habits the profile adds by itself.
- **Writing manner**: voice, length of messages, emoji and formality, with a preview of how the character sounds.
- **Status schedule**: a week grid of when the character is online, away, do not disturb, unavailable or offline, with a small random shift so changes are not exact to the minute.
- **Forum**: rank, signature, how often the character posts, whether they open their own threads and reply to you or to other characters, favourite boards.
- **Habits**: small quirks the profile adds by itself after talks and posts. You can add your own (marked "yours", not counted in the daily limit) or delete any.

Two actions sit at the top of the editor. **Fill empty** asks the model to propose values for the empty fields only; you see a list with checkboxes first and nothing changes until you press "Apply selected". **Update from Roleplay** shows what would change if the card was edited since the import. Leaving the editor with unsaved changes asks whether to save.
