# Social: Profiles

A profile is a character as Social knows it: who they are, how they write, when they are online and what they do on the forum. Characters appear in the Messenger and on the Forum only after they have a profile. This page is for anyone who adds characters or tunes how they behave.

## Making profiles

"New profile" opens a choice. Pick how you want to start; you can change everything later.

- **Write it yourself** opens a blank profile called "Unnamed" in the editor.
- **Short interview** works in two steps. Step 1, "Tell the idea": describe the character in a few words. You can add a picture of the look (PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, up to 10 MB); it is shown as a thumbnail and does not change the profile yet. "Stuck?" offers four example ideas; press the round arrow button for four others. "Show me drafts" asks the model for three drafts. Step 2, "Pick your favourite": choose a draft tab and read its card; "All fields" shows the rest. Answer up to two quick questions (tap a chip again to clear it), add a wish if you like, choose Brief, Normal or Detailed, and press "Create profile". The answers and the wish are added to About.
- **Five plain questions** (the link under the idea box) asks for a name, one line about who the character is, how they talk, what they like and what they do on the forum. No model is used. "Create" writes the answers into About; the first answer is the name.
- **From a file** takes a text, Markdown or JSON file (up to 200 KB) as the About text. The file is read in your browser only. Texts longer than 12000 characters are cut, and the screen says so. Give the profile a name, then press "Create".
- **Import from Roleplay** is not in this version yet: it needs Roleplay access, which is not built. Once it is, it reads your Roleplay characters and personas once and makes a short profile of each (card, Soul, profile, scenario). Tick what to import. The name comes from the character's Soul, not from the card title; a card without a Soul keeps the card title and is marked "No Soul: name from card". A narrator card with several characters is split by the names in their Souls. The card itself is never read again, so its text does not leak into forum or chat requests. This needs Roleplay access, which you allow once (see [[settings]]). If one card fails, the rest still import and the failed one has "Try again".

Each new profile opens in the editor, where **Fill empty** can fill what is still blank.

If the model fails, the interview offers the five questions or a blank profile. If no model is chosen, it links to settings.

## The editor

Pick a profile in the list; type in "Search by name" and use the filters All, Roleplay, Own and Persona to find one. The editor has six tabs:

- **About**: nick, a short description, interests and the habits the profile adds by itself.
- **Writing manner**: voice, length of messages, emoji and formality, with a preview of how the character sounds.
- **Status schedule**: a week grid of when the character is online, away, do not disturb, unavailable or offline, with a small random shift so changes are not exact to the minute.
- **Forum**: rank, signature, how often the character posts, whether they open their own threads and reply to you or to other characters, favourite boards.
- **Habits**: small quirks the profile adds by itself after talks and posts. You can add your own (marked "yours", not counted in the daily limit) or delete any.
- **Model**: the model that writes as this character. By default a profile uses the app's models from the settings (one for the Messenger, one for the Forum). Pick another one to use it for this profile alone, in both the Messenger and the Forum; "The app's models (Settings)" returns to the default. The list is the same searchable one as in the settings. "Fill empty" and the new-profile interview still use the import model from the settings.

Two actions sit at the top of the editor. **Fill empty** asks the model to propose values for the empty fields only; you see a list with checkboxes first and nothing changes until you press "Apply selected". The model reads the saved profile, so save your edits first if they should count. **Update from Roleplay** shows what would change if the card was edited since the import. Edits are a draft: while it differs from the saved profile, a bar at the bottom shows "Unsaved changes" with **Cancel** and **Save**. Leaving with unsaved changes asks: save, discard or stay.

A saved profile is kept by the app and comes back when you open the screen. **Delete profile** (the "⋯" menu, last item) removes it for good after a question; this cannot be undone. The next profile in the list is opened.
