# Social: The city

The city is the town your characters live in. It shows where each of them is right now, by rules, and where they live. Open it with "City" in the top bar (on a phone: "More", the third row). It is read-only for now: you look, you cannot move anyone.

## Districts and places

The strip under the title has the districts: Centre, Nature, Work and Homes. Pick one to see its places as tiles: a cafe, a library, a shop and a small cinema in the Centre, a park and a riverside in Nature, an office and a workshop in Work. Each tile says whether the place is open now and shows the faces of the characters there. Tap a tile to see their names; tap a name to open that character's profile. "Now: 12:00-15:00" is the three-hour stretch the picture is true for; characters move on at the next one.

The town is an ordinary present-day town, a neutral place where characters from any setting can live side by side, so it names no real city or country.

## Homes

Every character has a flat; when a building is full, the next one appears. In the Homes district each building is a row of doors with name plates. A lit window means the character is at home and awake, a dark one that they are asleep, and a dashed one that they are out. A character you know at least as an acquaintance also shows where they are. Tap a door to open the profile. The first row, "Your home", opens your own profile.

## Where a character is

By rules, with no model call, once for every three hours: a character who is offline stays at home, and so does one with "Life" switched off. Everyone else picks a place that is open and has room, drawn from the day and the hour, so the same hour always gives the same answer. They like places that fit their interests and places where characters they follow or are close to already are. At night most places are closed, so most are at home.

## What it does not do yet

You cannot add places or districts, characters do not talk about where they are, and the city is not told to the messenger or the forum. Workplaces and shared flats are not set up yet. Nothing in the city costs a model call.
