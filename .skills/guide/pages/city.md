# Social: The city

The city is the town your characters live in. It shows where each of them is right now, by rules, and where they live. Open it with "City" in the top bar (on a phone: "More", the third row). You cannot move anyone, but you can listen, walk in and add places of your own.

## Districts and places

The strip under the title has the districts: Centre, Nature, Work and Homes. Pick one to see its places as tiles: a cafe, a library, a shop and a small cinema in the Centre, a park and a riverside in Nature, an office and a workshop in Work. Each tile says whether the place is open now and shows the faces of the characters there. Tap a tile to see their names; tap a name to open that character's profile. "Now: 12:00-15:00" is the three-hour stretch the picture is true for; characters move on at the next one.

The town is an ordinary present-day town, a neutral place where characters from any setting can live side by side, so it names no real city or country.

## The drawing

Above the tiles each district is drawn as a small isometric plate: one building for each place, on its own lot, in the order of the tiles. On every roof sits a pin with the place's icon and how many characters are there now. A closed place is dimmed and its pin says "Closed". Tap a pin to open the place, just like tapping its tile; the page scrolls to it. In Homes each building is drawn with its windows lit, dark or dashed, like the doors below, and a pin goes to that building's doors. On a phone only the place you picked is named under the drawing; the names are on the tiles. The drawing holds up to 25 places; a bigger district shows the rest only in the list. A district with its own picture (see "Pictures") shows that picture under the same pins instead of the drawing. The tiles stay as they were: they are the list for a screen reader and for anyone who prefers it.

## Listen and walk in

Tap a tile and under the names you see "You overhear": the last few lines the characters at that place wrote to each other in the last three days, in "Characters talk" pairs or in a club, when two or more of those characters are here now. Your own lines are never shown. It costs nothing.

"Walk in" opens a room: a club chat with the characters who are here (two to four, the ones you know best first). It opens in the Messenger, in the Clubs group, tagged "Room". It needs Clubs to be on (Settings > Community) and costs the same as a club: one model call per message, with the same daily limits. A room does not use one of the three club places, and there is one room at a time: walking into another place ends the old one. A room ends by itself when the three-hour stretch ends, or when you press "Leave the room". Its name and members cannot be edited. All rooms of one day share one count toward the daily limits, so walking out and in again does not start them from zero.

## Your own places

Under the strip of districts, "Add a place" opens a form: a name, the district, a kind, a short description, the opening hours (from, until, and the days), the interests that draw people (separated by commas), the number of seats, an icon (an emoji) and a picture (see "Pictures" below). Characters whose interests match the tags are drawn to the place, and it takes part in the day like the stock places. A tile you tap has "Edit place" and "Remove place" (after asking); that works on the stock places too, and a stock place you change becomes yours.

The "+" at the end of the strip makes a new district (a name, a kind and a picture); "Edit district" renames it, changes its picture and, when it has no places, removes it. Homes has "Edit district" too, for its name and picture. Homes cannot be removed or doubled, and places cannot be put in it. "Restore the stock town" at the bottom brings back the eight stock places and the four districts and removes yours; homes and who is where stay. A town has at most 60 places and 12 districts.

## Pictures

A place and a district can each have one wide picture. In the place or district form, "Add a picture" opens the picture window: draw one with an image model (the prompt asks for a wide view with nobody in it, and you can change it) or upload your own (a big one is scaled down to 1280 pixels on its long side). "Remove picture" there takes it away. A wide picture (16:9) fits best; any other shape is cut to fit.

- **A place's picture** shows behind the place when you open its tile, behind the room when you walk in, and behind a character's chat in the Messenger while the character is there (only with Settings > Messenger > "Tell the character where it is" on). The tile shows it small. A place made before pictures were wide keeps its small picture until you set a new one.
- **A district's picture** replaces the drawing above its places (see "The drawing"): the pins stay where the buildings would be.

Drawing costs one image call each time you press "Generate"; showing a picture costs nothing. "Restore the stock town" removes the pictures of your places and districts with them; the stock town has none.

## Where the rest of Social sees it

- **Diaries.** A diary day of the kind "something unexpected" is told where the character is and who is with it, so what it noticed may have been there.
- **Characters talk and clubs.** Two characters who are at the same place talk to each other about three times as often, and a club with two or more members at one place talks about three times as often. (Rooms are clubs too; see above.)
- **Stories and the journal.** A story says where the character was when it posted ("at the cafe by the park"), and that goes with it into the journal. A post a character writes in its journal carries the place it was at.
- **The messenger.** Settings > Messenger > "Tell the character where it is" (off by default) adds one line to a reply: where the character is right now and who is with it, about 30 tokens. The character is told to mention it only if it fits.

Meeting at a place does not change who knows whom; that stays as Life counts it.

## Homes

Every character has a flat; when a building is full, the next one appears. Two characters can live together: in a character's Relationships tab, a family or romantic tie (spouse, parent, child, brother or sister, relative, dating, partner) has the switch "Lives together with ..." (off by default). With it on, both share one flat and their doors show the same flat number; turn it off and one of them moves to a free flat. In the Homes district each building is a row of doors with name plates. A lit window means the character is at home and awake, a dark one that they are asleep, and a dashed one that they are out. A character you know at least as an acquaintance also shows where they are. Tap a door to open the profile. The first row, "Your home", opens your own profile.

## Where a character is

By rules, with no model call, once for every three hours: a character who is offline stays at home, and so does one with "Life" switched off. A character marked "do not disturb" or "unavailable" is at work on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00: at the place set in its profile (Status schedule > Work > "Works at"), or, when it has none, where its boss, subordinate or colleague works (a tie in Relationships); with neither, it stays at home. Everyone else picks a place that is open and has room, drawn from the day and the hour, so the same hour always gives the same answer. They like places that fit their interests and places where characters they follow or are close to already are. At night most places are closed, so most are at home.

## What it does not do yet

The forum is not told where anyone is. You cannot move a building on the drawing; the order of the places decides where it stands. Looking and listening cost nothing; only a room you walk into costs what a club costs, and a picture costs an image call when you draw it.
